Privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Metro Haven Suites ("we", "us") respects your privacy and handles your personal information in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). This page explains what we collect when you use metrohavensuites.site, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Your name, mobile number and email address when you send a booking request.
  • Your stay details: suite, dates, number of guests and any message you send us.
  • Payment details you give us to confirm a booking: the payment method and reference number. We never ask for or store card numbers, bank logins or PINs.

Why we use it

  • To check availability, confirm or decline your request and send you booking emails.
  • To verify your payment and prepare your check-in.
  • To contact you about your stay, and to keep records required for accounting.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Building management of The Grass Residences, when needed for guest registration and security.
  • Trusted service providers that run this website for us: our booking database (Supabase) and email (Google). They only process your data to provide these services.
  • Government authorities, only when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep booking information only as long as needed for your stay, payment records and legal requirements, then delete it.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our admin page stores a sign-in session in the browser for our team only.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email us at metrohavensuites@gmail.com or message us on Messenger, and we'll reply within a few days. You may also contact the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).
